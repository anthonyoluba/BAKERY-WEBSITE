A working bakery, not a storefront for browsing

Royal Bakery doesn't do a display case or a menu board — it does bread, in serious volume, out the back door of a plain brick building on Fairview Street. Most regulars leave with six loaves or more, and some drive across town just to stock up.

No delivery, no cards — bring cash, and expect to be in and out fast. The entrance and parking are wheelchair accessible.
